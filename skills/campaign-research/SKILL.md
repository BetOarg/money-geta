# Campaign research

1. Research current public campaign pages; record URL and date/time checked.
2. Capture campaign owner, accepted platforms, payout formula, caps, remaining budget, eligible source content, content rules, attribution, approval process and payment terms.
3. Mark hidden, unavailable or login-gated values as UNKNOWN. Do not estimate rates or budgets.
4. Flag campaign terms that request leaks, piracy, early access circumvention, fake engagement or prohibited gameplay exploits.
5. Do not log in, join, accept terms, submit clips or spend money on the user's behalf.
6. Explain that earnings depend on accepted views, campaign rules, fraud review and payment terms; never promise income.
7. Ask for pasted rules when essential terms are behind login. Keep evidence links with each recorded fact.
