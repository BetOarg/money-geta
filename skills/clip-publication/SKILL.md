# Clip publication workflow

1. Trend radar selects a public source and records evidence.
2. Confirm reuse permission and attribution before editing.
3. Moment finder proposes timestamped candidates.
4. Clip cutter renders a candidate; preserve source files and create a new output path.
5. Hook writer proposes three factual hooks.
6. Campaign publication gate checks explicit rules and marks unresolved items INCOMPLETE.
7. Human reviews the full video, caption, credits, sources, factual claims, spoilers and rights.
8. Present final asset and caption. Wait for explicit approval. Never upload or publish automatically.

A technical pass does not establish copyright clearance, platform compliance or campaign acceptance.
