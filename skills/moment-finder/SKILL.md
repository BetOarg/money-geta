# Moment finder

1. Record the source URL/file, creator and retrieval date.
2. Use only signals actually available: timestamped transcript, audio peaks, replay heatmap or chat spikes. Mark unavailable signals as unavailable.
3. Return candidate start/end times, transcript evidence, signal evidence and confidence.
4. Reject suspected leaks, piracy, private material and prohibited content.
5. Recommend up to three candidates and explain why each may work as a short clip.
6. Do not infer permission from public availability. Confirm reuse rights before cutting or publishing.
7. After selection, hand off to clip-cutter and hook-writer. No auto-upload.
